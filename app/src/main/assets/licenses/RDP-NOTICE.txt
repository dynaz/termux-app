The built-in remote desktop of TermiNow (libterminow-rdp.so) contains:

FreeRDP 3.32.2 (commit 97eff6ddfbbfaa8e42a126d362686bd001ff8bdc)
  https://github.com/FreeRDP/FreeRDP
  Licensed under the Apache License, Version 2.0: see FreeRDP-LICENSE.txt.

OpenSSL 3.6.5 (commit c8bd5a57108599ac650bbae77fcabe3109dab2e8)
  https://github.com/openssl/openssl
  Licensed under the Apache License, Version 2.0: see OpenSSL-LICENSE.txt.

Both are built unmodified by native/rdp/build.sh of TermiNow, statically, with the JNI layer
native/rdp/jni/terminow_rdp.c of TermiNow. The Android client of FreeRDP (aFreeRDP, MPL-2.0)
is not used.
